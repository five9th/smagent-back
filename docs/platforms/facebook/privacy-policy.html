Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

This privacy policy applies to smagent_dev ("the application"), a personal automation tool used to publish posts to a Facebook Page that I own and manage, via the Facebook Graph API.

Scope

The application is a private, single-user tool. It is not offered to the public, and no other individuals can log in to it or use it. It is used exclusively by the developer to manage content on their own Facebook Page.

Data the application accesses

To publish posts and manage Pages I own, the application uses a Facebook Page access token with the following permissions:

  • business_management — to access Business settings and assets I own.
  • pages_show_list — to identify the Page(s) I manage.
  • pages_read_engagement — to read basic information and engagement data on my Page(s).
  • pages_read_user_content — to read content posted on my Page(s), used solely for context and moderation.
  • pages_manage_posts — to create and publish posts.
  • pages_manage_engagement — to manage engagement on my Page(s), such as moderating comments.
  • pages_manage_metadata — to manage my Page(s) settings and app subscriptions.

The application may access the Page's name and ID, and the content of posts it publishes. It does not collect or store personal data about other Facebook users or their profiles. Content posted by other users on my Page(s) may be read solely for context and moderation purposes and is not stored, analyzed, or shared.

How data is used and stored

Any data accessed (such as the Page ID and access token) is stored privately by the developer and used solely to publish and manage content on the developer's own Page(s) — including creating posts and moderating engagement. No data is sold, rented, shared with, or transferred to any third party. No data is used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than the Page management functionality described above.

Data retention and deletion

Access tokens and Page identifiers are retained only as long as the application is in use. The developer can delete this data at any time by removing the stored configuration. Deleting the application's authorization in Facebook Business Integrations revokes all access immediately.
