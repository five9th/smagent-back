Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This privacy policy applies to smagent_dev_personal and smagent_dev_company ("the applications"), two personal automation tools used to publish posts to my own LinkedIn profile and to a LinkedIn Page (organization) that I own and manage, via the LinkedIn API.

Scope

The applications are private, single-user tools. They are not offered to the public, and no other individuals can log in to them or use them. They are used exclusively by the developer to manage content on their own LinkedIn profile and their own LinkedIn Page.

Data the applications access

smagent_dev_personal — personal profile

The application uses a LinkedIn member access token of my own account with the following permissions:

  • w_member_social — to create and publish posts on my own profile.
  • openid and profile (optional) — to ask LinkedIn which member the token belongs to (via the userinfo endpoint) and to display my own name within the application.

The application may access my member identifier and my display name, and the content of posts it publishes.

smagent_dev_company — LinkedIn Page

The application uses an access token of my own account, acting on behalf of a LinkedIn Page I administer, with the following permissions:

  • w_organization_social — to create and publish posts on behalf of my Page.
  • r_organization_social_feed — to read engagement (such as comments) under posts published on my Page, so that I can review and respond to them.

The application may access the Page's identifier, the content of posts it publishes, and engagement on those posts, including the text of comments and the names of their authors as returned by the API. This data is read solely so that I can review and respond to engagement on my own posts; it is not stored beyond what is needed for that purpose, analyzed, profiled, or shared.

The applications do not collect or store personal data about other LinkedIn members or their profiles beyond what is described above, and do not access members' connections, messages, or any other private data.

How data is used and stored

Any data accessed (such as access tokens, my member identifier, my display name, and the Page identifier) is stored privately by the developer, with access tokens encrypted at rest, and used solely to publish and manage content on the developer's own profile and Page. No data is sold, rented, shared with, or transferred to any third party. No data is used for advertising, analytics, profiling, lead generation, or any purpose other than the publishing and engagement review functionality described above.

Data retention and deletion

Access tokens and identifiers are retained only as long as the applications are in use. The developer can delete this data at any time by removing the stored configuration. Removing the applications' authorization in LinkedIn's permitted services settings revokes all access immediately.
